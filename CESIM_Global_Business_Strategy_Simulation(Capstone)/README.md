# Project Overview

This repository presents our team's strategic decision-making process during the CESIM Global Challenge, a six-round business simulation where we managed a multinational company operating across USA, Asia, and Europe.

The project integrates:

- Business Strategy
- Operations Management
- Financial Decision Making
- Marketing Strategy
- Capacity Planning
- Supply Chain Decisions
- Competitive Analysis

# Key Strategic Decisions

## Pricing Strategy

- Low-price penetration in Asia
- Demand recovery through USA price optimization
- Europe relaunch with revised pricing

---

## Capacity Expansion

- Increased from 12 USA plants
- Expanded to 15 USA + 7 Asia plants
- Balanced production through dual-line allocation

---

## Operations Strategy

- Contract Manufacturing
- Internal Cash Transfer
- Capacity Optimization
- Demand Fulfillment

---

## Financial Strategy

- Profit Recovery
- Gross Margin Improvement
- Capital Allocation
- Shareholder Value Creation

---

# Key Learnings

✔ Capacity should match market demand

✔ Aggressive expansion can reduce profitability

✔ Pricing significantly affects market share

✔ Regional strategies should differ

✔ Dual-product portfolio improves risk diversification

✔ Internal cash transfers can improve operational efficiency
